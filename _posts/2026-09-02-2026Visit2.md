It's been tough schedule-wise for me to get back to the Fair this year, so I wasn't able to get in for my second visit until Wednesday, which officially started the second half of the Fair. I spent the afternoon and evening there for my annual solo visit.

Despite all of the things we did on our first visit, it felt like I'd barely been to the Fair at all this year, so I had a long list of goals. I started by quick-hitting three new foods that I'd wanted to try. 

I beelined for the food building and got the Sausage Sisters and Me When Pigs Fly sausage flight.

![When Pigs Fly Sausage Flight](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/pigs.webp)

This was a great start to the day! It was fun having four varieties of sausage and four different toppings in one food item, and I think they acually put some thought into the ordering of them as well - this is the same order I'd recommend eating them, sort of spicy-pallette cleanser-rich and spicy-tangy finish. The sausages were all clearly different and the toppings were all flavorful. My only nitpick here is that the stick is unnecessary and just makes eating it that much more difficult, since I don't think you'd want to actually try to eat these directly off of the stick. I have a pet peeve with food items that throw a stick in just so they can get in on the "on a stick!" Fair theme. 

Right next door in the next food stall is the new Saturday Dumpling Co. Their regular dumplings at their restaurants are fantastic, but I wanted to get something I could only get at the fair, so I got the Ube Cheesecake wontons. 

![Ube Cheesecake Wontons](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/ube.webp)

These might end up being the sleeper hit of the fair for me. Biting into one and seeing that bright purple filling is fun, and the twin toppings of sweetened condensed milk and crushed Biscoff were perfect in a "makes no sense but we're at the Fair so sure" sort of way. These were really good! Not too sweet, really well balanced actually. And if you've never had ube flavor before, this would be an excellent gateway food. 

After those wins, I headed over to try the Mini Donut Shake Up espresso drink from the Hamline Dining Hall's cafe counter. It's an unofficial new food this year, but quickly got a lot of buzz.

![Mini Donut Shakeup](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/shakeup.webp)


This was a pleasant way to get my afternoon caffiene fix on a really hot day at the fair. It tastes exactly as it sounds - cinnamon-y, sugar-y coffee, it's good and I'd definitely get it again.

Belly full, I wandered through the Creative Activities building. 

Loved this creative crocheted skeleton complete with internal organs!

![Crocheted Skeleton](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/skeleton.webp)

![Game-Themed Cakes](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/gamecakes.webp)

![Space Cookies #1](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/space1.webp)

![Ube Cheesecake Wontons](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/chocchip.webp)

![Murder Postcards](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/murder.webp)

I love that someone was passionate enough about this to go to the effort of collecting and presenting these!

After the art, I headed up to the North End. This year's display in the event center is about dinosaurs. These event center displays have swung between well-curated museum quality displays to clearly just here for the Instagram moment fluff. Happy to say that this year's display trended more towards the former. 

![Dinos](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/dinos.webp)
![More Dinos](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/dinos2.webp)

It seemed to be specifically Cretaceous-era dinosaurs (perhaps due to space restrictions) but there were several life-size dinosaur fossil castings and lots of informative displays. Plus, air conditioning! 

On my way back down from the North End I stopped at the new Summer Lakes Boat House to try their expanded menu. I got the riblets and chips. 

![Riblets and Chips](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/riblet.webp)

I'd give this a solid B. The riblets were really tender and well-sauced, maybe just a touch on the dry side. The chips are homemade and if you like thin chips you'd probably like these. I'm more of a kettle chip fan. This was also pricey for the amount of food. 

I had better luck with their mocktails which is what they were originally known for at the Fair. This is the lemon sweet tea mocktail (mixed with seltzer water). 

![Lemon Sweet Tea Mocktail](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/mocktail.webp)

It was really good and refreshing in the heat! Would definitely get this or another of their mocktails again.

I decided to swing by the Ag Bldg on the off chance that the crop art line wasn't insane (spoiler: it was). So instead of crop art I went through the scarecrows. Here's a gem that only local Minnesotans will understand:

![Guaranteed](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/guaranteed.webp)

I also popped through the christmas trees, and was delighted to find this Star Trek themed tree complete with the iconic moments from the original series, including tribbles and the Kirk-Uhura kiss, the first scripted interracial kiss on TV. (Keep in mind that until one year earlier, interracial marraige was still technically illegal in the US.)

![Star Trek Tree](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/trek.webp)

It was only getting hotter out as the afternoon went on, so I needed some ice cream. I tried the Dairy Goodness Bar's flavor of the year, the Moo-sota Apple Pie. One professional fair reviewer declared that she had tried the flavor of the year every year of her adult life and that this was the best one ever. Strong praise!

![Moo-sota Apple Pie Sundae](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/moosota.webp)

I dunno about best ever, but definitely really darn good! I have fond memories of the Big Apple from Ted Drewes Frozen Custard in St. Louis, and this is not that (nothing could be) but it's definitely reminiscent of it. Big chunks of apple and lots of crumbled crust bits.

I enjoyed my sundae while having a moment of zen at the DNR fish pond watching the paddlefish swim in circles.

![Paddlefish](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/paddle.webp)

From there, it was time for the FFA lama costume contest! It features middle and high schoolers designing a head-to-toe costume both for themselves and their animal (llama or alpaca, I think mostly alpacas?), and then training them to wear and show it. This is the cult favorite event of the fair, and they recently had to move to the Coliseum to handle the large crowds. I got there fifteen minutes early and still was relegated to the nosebleeds, and by showtime the entire Coliseum was full and standing room only. Despite the Coliseum's extensive renovation, the A/C wasn't up to the task of cooling this crowd, so I was sweating, but managed to last through the first two classes. This was my favorite one, a Back to School theme. 

![School Bus Themed Alpaca](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/school.webp)

I headed out to cool off and pick up a treat from Roon's Savory, a new vendor. Their deal is savory foods in waffle cones. I tried the chicken salad one which was getting good reviews. 

![Roon's Savories Chicken Salad](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/roons.webp)

This was... fine. I suppose the cone made it possible to eat while walking, but otherwise, didn't really do much for me. Chicken salad itself was decent, and it was definitely a nice break from fried fair treats, but I'd probably not get it again.

I had time to kill before a concert I wanted to see so I went to the Lumberjack Show which I hadn't seen in ages. 

![Lumberjack Show](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/lumberjack.webp)

It's definitely light on content - they probably could have done the whole show in ten minutes. There's a lot of filler and fluff, and honestly, this isn't that interesting of a show. Definitely skippable, but if you want to rest for 30 minutes, it's fine.

I headed to the International Bazaar stage for the concert, but made a last-minute call to suffer through the crop art line first, figuring that even with a 30 minute wait this might be the best opportunity I was going to get all Fair.

![Crop Art Line](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/artline.webp)

![Mini Donut Beer](/assets/2026/2026-09-02/beer.webp)

Just another great free Fair concert. I think the live music scene is one of the most underrated aspects of the Fair - there were 55 free live music events on Wednesday alone! You could easily do an entire day at the Fair just going from concert to concert and still feel like you missed half the bands you wanted to see.

The concert was the perfect way to close out another successful solo visit to the fair. With sunset there finally was a bit of a cool breeze, and it was nice to just sit and absorb the music and watch all the people having a great time dancing away to the groovy beats. 

I finally headed out to the shuttle bus, exhausted but feeling happy that I was able to fit in my annual solo trip. The Fair seems to be flying by and I'm definitely feeling FOMO. Happy to be going back again on Friday with Katie!
