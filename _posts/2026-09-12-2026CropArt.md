I teased the crop art earlier in the Fair run, and I also shared some in my post about Minnesota's difficult year, but I promised a full crop art roundup post, and so you shall get a full crop art roundup post!

There was big, big news in the crop art world this year!

[![seedrules](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/seedrules.webp)](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/seedrules.webp)

That's right: QUINOA, CHIA and CARDAMOM BANNED!!

The seeds are being banned because [Minnesota farmers don't grow them](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/04/17/mn-state-fair-crop-art-new-rules). The contest organizers are making an effort to get back to the crop art's roots in promoting Minnesota agriculture. 

Apparently there's still hope. Mustard seeds were banned in 2023, but apparently allowed back in the following year?!

[![stayburied](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/stayburied.webp)](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/stayburied.webp)

The next one needs some explanation, although really its inexplicable. See, there's a local lake in Minneapolis called Lake of the Isles. And one of the houses on that lake has a giant pencil in its front yard. And that pencil gets sharpened every year. And that sharpening has become a [huge cult celebration attended by thousands](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/06/06/thousands-celebrate-5th-giant-pencil-sharpening-on-lake-of-the-isles) known as The Sharpening. It's all very Minneapolis. And as you can see here, yes full grown adults dress up as pencils and perform, and the pencil is of course sharpened with a giant sharpener.

[![pencil2](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/pencil2.webp)](/assets/2026/2026CropArt/pencil2.webp)
