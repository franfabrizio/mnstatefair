Katie and I took a day off of work and headed to the Fair on Friday for our annual date day visit. The weather was not ideal, as the heat index was approaching 100, but the Fair waits for no one, so into the furnace we headed!

Remember the Great French Fry Controversy from Visit 1? Well, the Fresh French Fry folks pivoted quickly after the whiplash and the original potatoes were back by the end of day two. Which was lucky for us, because Katie was the recipient of a token to get free french fries! The company that runs the stand recently moved into Katie's office building and gave them to all of the other tenants! (Katie hasn't asked them about the controversy yet - we figure its too soon).

Token in hand, we headed there straight from the busses to begin our date day. 

[![Fresh French Fries](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/fries.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/fries.webp)

The fries are back to normal and the world is right again. Phew.

From there it was off to the races. Sticking in the fries food group, we got a tip from our friend Michelle to try the pickle fries from Mike's as an alternative to the Perfect Pickle. It's gonna be tough to beat the Perfect Pickle, but we gave it a go: 

[![Pickle Fries](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/picklefries.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/picklefries.webp)

These were good! They're not a replacement for Perfect Pickle, but the way I described it is that I'd be perfectly happy replacing _one_ of my Perfect Pickle orders in a year with these. Crispy more than crunchy, and the dipping sauce has just a touch of heat which was nice.

Next, we headed to Dino's. I wanted Katie to try one of my favorite new food from last year, the Athena Whipped Feta. I'm glad they brought them back this year, this should be an every year item.

[![Whipped Feta](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/feta.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/feta.webp)

So good again! Spicier than last year, had to be careful not to get too much of the green stuff in any one bite. The fried pita is really nicely done, too.

We made a great find at Dino's - I knew about their patio seating, but had no idea there's a "secret" back room. It's like an oasis from the Fair, very few people seem to know it's there! It even has some lovely murals.

[![Dino's](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/dinos.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/dinos.webp)

Katie went next door and picked up a grilled peach to complement our feta. She opted for the Peach Pie Parfait bowl, which comes with greek yogurt and pie crumbles.

[![Grilled Peach](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/peach.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/peach.webp)

Delicious! A warm grilled peach is a great palette cleanser and refreshing treat. It was a great way to get out of the heat for a while.

Bellies full, we headed over to the Ag Building to check on some giant veggies. On the way we ran into the parade. 

[![Parade](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/parade.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/parade.webp)


Parades are weird.

We got distracted a couple of times in the Ag Building. First, by the apples:

[![Apples](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/apples.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/apples.webp)

Since we were there, we picked up a couple of cider freeze pops (which I forgot to photograph). Again, 100 degree heat index, so these were perfection. And still $3, one of the best values at the Fair!

Then we wandered into an event I've never attended before, the bonsai contest! Fun to still be discovering new things at the Fair after all of these years.

[![Bonsai Tree #1](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai1.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai1.webp)

The entry cards listed how long the tree had been in training. Some of the competitors have been training their trees for 30 years!

[![Bonsai Tree #2](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai2.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai2.webp)

I liked this unusual one a lot:

[![Bonsai Tree #3](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai3.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/bonsai3.webp)

From there we finally made it to the vegetable competition. 

[![Garlic](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/garlic.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/garlic.webp)

We get a CSA veggie box every week, and one week we got an absolutely enormous turnip - like the size of my head enormous. Turns out, it could have been worse:

[![Giant Turnip](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/turnip.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/turnip.webp)

I also have a large veggie garden, and this year I've been innundated with tomatillos. I think I see a ribbon opportunity next year - not a ton of entries! 

[![Tomatillos](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/tomatillos.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/tomatillos.webp)

After wandering the Ag Building for quite a while, we crossed the street for our favorite Fair food item: the Peterson's Pork Chop!

[![Pork Chop](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/chop.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/chop.webp)

This was the best tasting version of this in years, which is really saying something! So, so, so good. I could have eaten three of these. However, they have gotten both thinner and more expensive. Boo inflation/shrinkflation! It's ludicrous to spend $13 on a pork chop, but this chop's taste is also ludicrous, so I'll keep throwing my wallet at this booth every year regardless.

We needed to get out of the sun and rest, so we checked the daily schedule. What's this? FFA Safe Tractor Operation Competition?! Let's go!

On the way down to Compeer Arena, we passed by the Chocolate Milk Drinking Contest. Kudos to these people who decided to chug milk in this heat. 

[![Chocolate Milk Contest](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/chocolatemilk.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/chocolatemilk.webp)

I especially like contestant #3, who is posing like this is what she does every day at 3pm.

So, about that tractor contest. Fair organizers: you might want to let folks know that the first part of the FFA Safe Tractor Operation Contest is.... a five-page written test. Not the best spectator sport: 

[![Tractor Test](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/test.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/test.webp)

Also, the dude whose legs are in the lower right of this picture was cheating off of the dude in the yellow tee shirt. Just sayin'.

Then, Fair Organizers, you might also want to let folks know that the second part of the contest is... a very thorough orientation to the tractor for all of the contestants... twice, because they split the group up. Oh well, we were out of the sun at least, and near Bridgeman's, so Katie grabbed a Gray Duck Sundae.

[![Ice Cream](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/grayduck.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/grayduck.webp)

This was a Katie favorite from previous years. Licorice flavored ice cream with whipped cream that is hiding my favorite part: a generous sprinkling of those tiny, crunchy marshmallows like you get in hot chocolate packets. Yum.

From there, though, the tractor action was riveting! We were on the edge of our seats as the contestants tried to maneuver their tractors and trailers without knocking off any of the golf balls on top of the poles.

[![Tractor Maneuvering](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/tractor.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/tractor.webp)

Just kidding. This was all unfolding at 0.1 MPH. This guy took like five minutes to back this trailer in. He did get it in without knocking over any golf balls though!
It was so. Slow. To. Watch. I'm not sure why they even advertise this as a public event! But, it was a good respite.

It was MPR Day at the Fair, which means that MPR was responsible for all of the programming in Dan Patch Park. We wandered over there, found a shady patch of grass, and thoroughly enjoyed the concert from Davina and the Vagabonds.

[![Davina and the Vagabonds](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/davina.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/davina.webp)

I don't know how to really describe their music. Americana/Jazz/Pop/Dunno? Look them up, they're great!

After some Grandstand shopping (way too hot inside, cut it short), we started heading towards Creative Activities. On the way, we passed the most Fair Fair thing I've seen in a while. Where on Earth are you ever going to see a polka band doing a mashup cover of Backstreet Boys and The White Stripes in front of an AFL-CIO union pavillion? At the Fair, of course! (Sorry for the video quality, I was facing directly into the sun)

I popped into the Fair gift shop and found a fun new coffee mug. I am very, very particular about the shape of coffee mugs, and this is the ideal mug!

Just out in front of the shop is this year's most popular new vendor: Minnesota Apple Fries. The line was long but moved really fast - they seem to have optimized apple fry delivery. 

[![Apple Fries](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/applefries.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/applefries.webp)

These were excellent! One of those items that makes you think, "why hasn't someone done this before?" Fried Minnesota apples at the MN state fair is a no-brainer, and I'm glad they're here now. This feels like it'll be the next Fair classic food. 

One thing, though - I'm worried about the workers' long-term lung health! They have this crazy barrel contraption that they fill with powdered sugar and toss the fries into to coat. This thing is spewing out clouds of powdered sugar, and everything, I mean everything in the vicinity is coated with a think layer of it. Everyone's clothes, everyone's hair, every surface. No one was wearing a mask, which means they're breathing this in all day long. Hopefully they get that sorted out next year! 

In Creative Activities, we wandered through some sections that neither of us had seen yet this year in the back of the building. Saw some fun things! I liked this dragon in what appeared to be an entire dragon kid's doll category.

[![Dragon Craft](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/dragon.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/dragon.webp)

I also wanted to show Katie the murder postcards collection I liked so much. I hadn't noticed this very funny one before:

[![Murder Postcard](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/modernmurder.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/modernmurder.webp)

However, newsflash: I have a new favorite most random collection of things. I present to you A Collection of Patterns Found Inside Security Envelopes.

[![Security Envelopes](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/patterns.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/patterns.webp)

I love this so much! The detail, the categories, the "this display features *a few of my favorites*"... Lauren Bowe, you are a Minnesota treasure!

We also checked out the cupcake competition. Spongebob was a strong entry!

[![Cupcakes](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/cupcakes.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/cupcakes.webp)

The depth of entries in each specific baking category is impressive.

[![Cookie Contest](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/cookiecontest.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/cookiecontest.webp)

Check out this model of the Fallingwater, the Frank Lloyd Wright house!

[![House on the Rock](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/fallingwater.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/fallingwater.webp)

Katie and I both weirdly had a craving for pickle pizza. I've avoided the pickle pizza hype since trying it the first year, but for watever reason it sounded really good to me, so I hopped in the line while Katie finished up in Creative Activities. 

We grabbed two slices and found a quiet spot near the MnDOT snowplow.

[![Pickle Pizza](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/picklepizza.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/picklepizza.webp)

This was WAY better than I remembered it. The slice is large and the crust is legit! Flavor-wise, I have to say, it's actually pretty good. I don't remember it being this good. The line is still too long for this, but if you catch in on a shorter day, you could do a lot worse than this.

We enjoyed some downtime in this quiet corner of the Fair. 

[![Smile](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/smile.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/smile.webp)

Nearby was a MnDOT trivia sign. Did you know Minnesota has the 4th largest public road network in America? (California, Texas, Illinois)

They had a snowplow you could check out.

[![Snowplow](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/snowplow.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/snowplow.webp)

All we can say is: So. Many. Buttons! Holy cow. Literally like 70 buttons. And terrible visibility. Give the plows lots of room, folks! We got to talk to a MnDOT plow driver from North Branch. He told us about working the interstate 35W closure last year in the February ice storm and about how he was hit by a semi in a blizzard three years ago. A dangerous and important job, thanks to all you plow drivers!

Our last stop on our date day visit was the West End. Katie checked out all of the shops, and I got the new peach soft serve ice cream at the Prye's stand next to LuLu's.

[![Peach Soft Serve Beer](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/peachbeer.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/peachbeer.webp)

You'll recall from last year the crazy buzz around the soft serve ice cream. They added peach to the original raspberry flavor this year. This was still good, but a little less "OMG that's beer ice cream" than the raspberry was. Either because the novelty is less this year or the peach flavor made it seem more like ice cream. Anyway, good not great.

I also caught the Foci glass blowing demonstration for the first time in many, many years.

[![Glass Chicken](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/glass.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit3/glass.webp)

They were working on a large chicken. In this heat. Oof.

Speaking of the heat, after a long day we were ready to head home. Despite the weather, another super fun date day at the Fair. We covered a lot of ground and ate a lot of good food as always. 

To wrap this up, I'll share a few overheard conversations that were very Fair!

Group of teens trying to look cool wandering by in a pack. One guy says to another: "You've never been in the Butterfly House? Oh man, it's so sick! They come right up to you!"

Two men at the cookie display: "No, see, I like my chocolate chip cookies with a little height to them. You've gotta have the height to ensure a gooey center. Like, see, here, Harold and Laura (points to two entries), they know where it's at."

Guy looking at the baking display: "They've got marzipan up in here? Who's still cooking with marzipan? I mean, marzipan's ok, but it's worse than literally every other dessert."

Ah, good times at the Great Minnesota Get-Together! We'll be back one more time for our family labor day visit. See you then!
