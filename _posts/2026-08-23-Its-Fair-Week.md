Summer has gone by in the blink of an eye and I'm definitely not ready for the Fair! (Didn't I say the same thing last year?) Feeling late to the party, but I have a few days to catch up and get prepared. Our first visit this year will probably be Friday, not Thursday. I'm bummed to miss the first-day vibes, but Friday works better for us.

I'm going to mix it up a bit this year and do a quick-hit preview post. Let's get to it!

* Butter Brew Mustache Pretzel (Blue Moon)
* Bao Belly (RC's BBQ)
* When Pigs Fly  (Sausage Sister & Me)
* Apple Donut Ham Grinder (Farmer's Union)
* Peri Peri Bowl (Afro Deli)
* Jive Turkey BBQ (New Vendor)
* Roon's Savories (New Vendor)
* Saturday Dumpling Co. (New Vendor)
* Summer Lakes Boat House (New Vendor)

New Foods I'm Not Excited to Try (but might anyway - for science)

* Surf n Turf Burger (Cafe Caribe)
* Walking Chopped Italian Grinder (Mancini's)
* Elote Tots (Tot Boss)
* Dill With It Cookie (Urban Glow Mocktails)
* The Fried and the Furious: Cubano Drift (Herbivorous Butcher)

New Alcoholic Drinks I'm Excited to Try

* Tropical Boba Burst Hazy (Cafe Caribe - brewed by Pryes)
* Ol' Fashioned Hard Seltzer Cocktail (Coasters - brewed by Lift Bridge)
* First Place Peach (Lulu's - brewed by Pen Name Fermentation Project)
* Soft Serve Royal Peach Beer (Prye's Brewing Pavillion) (Is it a drink? Is it ice cream? Still not sure.)

New Alcoholic Drinks I'm Not Excited to Try

* Homer Black Pepper IPA (Ballpark - brewed by Surly)
* Lime in the Coconut Cream Ale (Giggle's - brewed by Millstream)
* Everything new at the Hideaway Speakeasy (Quacker Jack Seltzer, Pickle & Vine, Boozy Rose Lemonade)

Grandstand Show Thoughts

* Almost none are exciting. Again.
* Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis Homecoming: A Celebration of the Minneapolis Sound is probably going to be really good.
* Hairball (the free replacement for the canceled Rod Stewart) would be fun.

Other New Things I'm Excited About

* Films at the Fair - MN-made short film screenings
* Checking out the Coliseum's renovation
* The new Eat Together area near the Giant Sing Along
* The new Summer Lakes Boat House
* The "50 Years of State Fair Recipes" cookbook
* The official design artwork looks nice this year. Still miss the posters though.

Alright, gear up, put on your most comfortable shoes, and lets do this thing! Look for my first daily report Friday night.
