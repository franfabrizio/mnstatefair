Wow. We hit the fair hard yesterday - or more accurately, the fair hit us hard yesterday. We clearly are not in game day shape this year, because it was EX-HAUST-ING. Eight hours at the Fair just about did us in. I've finally recovered enough to put together the blog post, but I still need a nap!

Having said that, even though it doesn't feel like it, we covered a lot of ground and had a lot of fun there yesterday!Let's get into it.

Henry has been ready for the Fair for a few days now, but Katie and I both felt like we were unprepared and rushing headlong into it after a busy summer. Nevertheless, we managed to get ourselves to the Bloomington park and ride by 7:40am or so and hit the line for the busses. We beat the rush and were on the Fairgrounds by about 8:30, and as is the case every year, once we step inside and see that beautiful State Fair arch, the excitement returned and we were raring to go!

![Obligatory Annual Family Selfie](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/statefair.webp)

It did seem more crowded than usual for that time of the morning, which would foreshadow big crowds later in the day. Thursday CRUSHED the all time day one attendance record by nearly 20k people as well. The weather both days was picture perfect so that contributed, but probably even more than that is the late Labor Day. A lot more school systems are starting school here before Labor Day this year (welcome to our world, rest of MN - Edina's been doing this for years now) so I think a lot of families are front-loading their visits this year. I won't be surprised if yesterday was another record.

The first order of business was coffee. My first choice is always the nitro cold press at Farmer's Union, but right now the priority was whatever was close, which was Java Jive. Maybe I've been underappreciating them, because my vanilla latte was really dang good. Henry also gave the Chai Blender high marks. 

![Morning Coffee](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/coffee.webp)


After that, breakfast. Pork belly is essentially bacon on steroids, so that definitely counts for breakfast and at 9:01am we got one of the first servings of the new Pork Belly Bao Bun at RC's BBQ. 

![Rc BBQ Bao Buns](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/baobun.webp)

Henry housed most of one while Katie and I split the other, and we all really liked this. This has gotten mixed reviews - some folks have loved it and ranked it among the best new foods, while others felt the pork belly was underdone. I dunno, I thought the pork was just about perfectly done, so maybe there's a consistency issue, or maybe Minnesota doesn't fully appreciate pork belly, but we gave this a strong thumbs' up.

To round out breakfast, we visited Greater Tater for some breakfast skillet tots. I wrote about Greater Tater last year as a new vendor, and I'm sure we'll be making this an annual stop. These were yummy!

![Greater Tater](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/taters.webp)

After breakfast, Henry was all about the sheep! The barns were still cool and fresh in the morning so it was a good time to visit our farm friends. 

![Excited for Sheep](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/excited.webp)

After checking out the sheep haircut scene, we settled in for some ram and ewe judging. Seems like the common denominator for a winning sheep is 1. big for your breed, 2. straight back, 3. vibes. The ewe judging got a little weird when the judge started describing with phrases like "a really strong group of robust females, they've got what you're looking for, these girls are stout and really nice through their front thirds..." 

![Sheep Judging](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/sheep.webp)

After the sheep we checked in on the new coliseum renovations and then headed into the Miracle of Birth barn before it got too crazy in there. We're still early so not a ton of babies yet, but we did get to see a 6 hour old calf, a day-old lamb, a just-born litter of 17 (!) piglets, and some duckling and chick fluffballs.

![Lamb](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/lamb.webp)

![Petting the Lamb](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/pet.webp)


![Piglets](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/piglets.webp)

Next up was the cow barn. We had a nice conversation with one of the exhibitors, who told us that they bought what turned out to be a champion cow by accident and almost got rid of her because she was so awkward in her "teenage" years. We're going to check back later in the fair to see how she did in her shows this year.

![Cow](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/cow.webp)

It's always fun to run into friends at the Fair. Katie has an amazing radar for spotting folks in the crowd, and we ran into her childhood penpal and her family. This is the second time in a year we've bumped into them after decades of not seeing each other!

![Pen Pal](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/penpal.webp)

We also caught up with our friends Ben, Melissa, Abby and Emily, who had the foresight to text us just in case we were at the fair (always a decent chance!) We had a brief catch-up outside the cow barn, where their expertise at finding free things came in handy as we got lots of great tips!

![Fimans](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/fimans.webp)

From there, what started out as just the three of us for the morning hours turned into an extended family day as we met up with the Richards clan. With several more folks on hand, we had enough mouths to really get into the food sampling for real.

![Group Shot](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/group.webp)

First up were corn dogs. Pronto pup or pancho dog? How about both! I secured the pancho dogs while the gang waited in line for the pronto pups. Katie and I are still team pancho while Henry rides or dies with the pronto pup.

![Pancho Dogs](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/pancho.webp)
![Pronto Pup](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/pronto.webp)

How do you wash down a hot dog wrapped in fried batter? With a slab of big fat bacon, obvs.

![Big Fat Bacon](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/bacon.webp)

Palette cleansers were definitely in order after that. Katie and Ada opted for the new Sweet Peach Lemonade at Quench'd. 

![Sweet Peach Lemonade](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/lemonade.webp)

Meanwhile, proud moment - I got to introduce my niece Eleanor to her first mini donut beer!

![Mini Donut Beer](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/beer.webp)


What's that? We're just outside the food building? Oh, I guess that means we need cheese curds. Mission accomplished. Perfect as always. Mouth Trap is the single most consistently excellent food item at the Fair. 

![Mouth Trap Curds](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/curds.webp)

We took our curds over to the quiet patch of grass near the Admin Building and settled in to eat while listening to several bangers from  the California Dreamin' - the Sounds of Laurel Canyon free concert at the bandshell across the street.

After watching the Space Tower go up and down a dozen times, several of us decided to give it a spin. Looking good, Twin Cities!

![Space Tower](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/tower.webp)
![View](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/view.webp)


Katie wanted to hit the Creative Activities bulding so we headed up that way. I stopped into O'Garas to try another new item. Longtime readers will know how I feel about O'Gara's consistently terrible attempts at food, but I was intrigued by this year's offering: Sligo slider bites, basically cheeseburger fillings in dough on a stick with Thousand Island dipping sauce. They were described as both the dumplings and the dressing being handmade, so maybe they learned their lesson? Worth a try. 

![Sliders](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/sliders.webp)

Ok, this is going to be controversial. Reviewers have mostly panned these as bland and dense. Bah humbug. I liked these a lot! They gave off White Castle "onion'y squishy burger" vibes, which happens to be a guilty pleaure of mine. Were they actually homemade? Was that not just Kraft Thousand Island dressing? I don't know. I just know I enjoyed an O'Gara's food item for once.

Henry and I ate these while resting our feet and watching Cafe Latte cook in the demonstration kitchen. While being a regular Cafe Latte cake-eater, I haven't had an actual meal there in forever. It looked yummy, I need to get back there sometime soon!

I didn't actually make it to any of the Creative Activities browsing, so I'll save that for a future visit, but from what I saw of Katie's pictures, looks like a strong year!

We were within striking distance of Baba's and the siren call of those pillowy, warm, delicious pita puffs, so off we went. However, ROOKIE MISTAKE! No picture of the Baba's! You've seen their hummus bowls here in the past. This year we tried the Hummus Bi Lahme bowl (spiced beef and fairly traditional toppings). Delicious, as always. The new "Eat Together" space they've been touting is basically just a bunch of tables in a grassy area, but it is across the path from the Giant Sing Along so you can hear your fellow fairgoers belt out / butcher the classics while you eat. 

![Happy Place](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/happyplace.webp)

With cousins around, it was time for rides (so that the parents don't have to go on them with Henry!), so we moseyed towards the Midway. Which brings us right past the corn roast, so...

![Katie Corn](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/katiecorn.webp)

I was worried about the corn this year because of the drought conditions, but in Untiedt's (the corn provider) we should trust, because there was nothing to worry about, it's fantastic.

To the Midway! The cousins took on the Alpine Bobs followed by the swings, which I actually couldn't watch because I hate amusement park swings with a passion. Then Ada and Henry challenged the Kraken and lived to tell about it! Henry's hat almost didn't, as he forgot to take it off and it got jeeted off of his head mid-ride, but in a stroke of incredible luck, it landed right on the platform. 

![Alpine Bobs](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/alpine.webp)
![Swings](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/swings.webp)

Exiting the Midway the Fabrizios were really starting to drag. This is how we felt. 

![Poop](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/poop.webp)

So a pick-me-up cocktail was in order. I tried the new Lift Bridge Ol' Fashioned Hard Seltzer Cocktail from Coasters. 

![Cocktail](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/cocktail.webp)

Definitely gave off Old Fashioned vibes, but for me, it wasn't quite hitting right. It was fine, especially as it got colder with all the ice, but I don't think I'd get it again. On the other hand, Katie declared it as the best drink she's ever had at the Fair. Strong endorsement! She felt it had licorice undertones, which she loves and might explain why I didn't, not a licorice guy. 

Henry was, as he would say, "poofed" by this point, so we needed to think about wrapping up the day. All that was left were a couple of Fair classics, Sweet Martha's and Fresh French Fries. Buckets obtained, we found a grassy patch to rest and enjoy. The cookies were exactly as expected - a warm gooey mess of mediocre chocolate chip cookies. 

![Cookies](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/cookies.webp)

The fries, however... oh boy.

![Fries](/assets/2026/2026Visit1/fries.webp)

There were dark rumors swirling after day one that they changed the fries. That can't be, it's a classic, it's a winner, you don't mess with the fries. We had to find out for ourselves. 

THEY. CHANGED. THE. FRIES!

Word on the street is that they switched out the local potatoes from Big Lake, MN with Colorado potatoes. And wow, was it obvious. Where were the dark potato skins, the fluffy and floppy interiors, the bits of odds and ends on the bottom of the cup? What is this abomination of homogenous, pale/yellow'y, super starchy, thick and stiff fries??

Shocking. I cannot believe they messed with their golden goose. Oh, this is gonna get ugly.

I can't wait to see how this unfolds. Was it a supply issue? Are they trying to cut corners? And the plot thickens.... as of last night, my [reddit investigations]( https://www.reddit.com/r/minnesotastatefair/comments/1w0d3pg/they_ruined_the_french_fries/) hint that the Big Lake potatoes might be making a comeback. Some people are claiming that they started pumping out the OG fries as of late yesterday afternoon. Did they quickly learn their lesson and pivot back? Is the damage done? Time will tell.

At this point we were all exhausted. We bid our farewells and headed to the exit. At the last minute, I remembered I wanted to look for the new State Fair Blue Ribbon Winners cookbook, and I quickly secured it at the merch tent. Pretty excited about this, lots of great looking recipes to try!

The moment we made it onto the bus Henry crashed out - I've never seen him so tired in the middle of the afternoon! He spent most of the ride laying on one or the other of us. We made it home around dinnertime, and then all of us just fell apart. Henry zoned out in front of the TV for hours, I did the same downstairs, and Katie slept straight through till 9:30pm. Oof. I haven't felt this beat after a Fair visit in a long time. Part of it was bad prep - Henry and I will both be shopping for some new sneakers this weekend! But I'm still surprised with how tiring that was. Gotta get back into Fair shape ASAP!

Next visit is up in the air, it'll probably be my solo visit sometime in the first half of next week. Katie and I are planning to go back next Friday, and then possibly a Labor Day visit. Might be a bit of a light year for the Fair for us, but I'll try to cover as much ground as I can the next week and a half!
