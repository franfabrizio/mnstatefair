You would think with three full visits to the Fair we would have covered all of the ground we wanted to, but not even close. We were feeling some serious FOMO and decided that yes, we really did need the Labor Day visit again this year. We'll just go for a few hours and hit the top things and make it a quick trip. Right?

We hadn't seen any of the horse events yet and the rodeo finals are always on the last day, so we headed straight to them when we got in. The barrel racing was intense!

[![Barrel Racing](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/racer.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/racer.webp)

[![Barrel Racing 2](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/rider2.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/rider1.webp)

I made a couple of food runs during the show. I got ambitious and tried to see if I could sneak over to Peachy's Donuts early. Ha! Yeah, the line was two blocks long. Detour to Farmer's Union for a Maple Cream Nitro Cold Brew while I formulate a Plan B.

[![Nitro Coffee](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/nitro.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/nitro.webp)

I still remember when Farmer's Union introduced this, it was like a revelation because it was the first coffee I considered good at the Fair. Now there are other choices, but the OG still hits the spot.

Plan B was to get Henry his requested steak tip dinner from Pickle Barrel (yes, it's before 10am) on the way back and then get some breakfast items near the Coliseum. The steak tip dinner never photographs well.

[![Barrel Racing](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/steakdinner.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/steakdinner.webp)

Don't be fooled, though. Every part of this is delicious, especially those 'shrooms! We had no problem polishing this off between the three of us, but this is the smaller of the two sizes and it's still a ton of food. 

I also popped just outside to Buni's Cinnamon Rolls. We have always heard that Cinnesmith's has the best so we generally go there, but it's inconveniently all the way at the north end of the Fair. Figured I'd give these a try, I opted for the pecan caramel roll. 

[![Pecan Caramel Roll](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/bunis.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/bunis.webp)

This was amazing! Really liked it. I dunno why Buni's doesn't get as much love as Cinnesmith's. I'd call this better in the cinnamon roll category, unless you specifically like the mini ones Cinnesmith's does, which are easier to eat while walking around. 

We also got Greater Tater's new buffalo chicken tots. 

[![Greater Tater](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/tots.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/tots.webp)

These were just ok, they'll be a one-and-done for us. Skippable, but we like to support Greater Tater (owned by an individual with Down syndrome!)

Yes, I realize this was a rather weird mix of items for breakfast. There are no rules at the Fair. 

We finished up our time at the horse show with the pole weaving race. I caught a fun three-picture sequence from one of the contestants: 

[![Pole Weaving 1](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole1.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole1.webp)

[![Pole Weaving 2](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole2.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole2.webp)

[![Pole Weaving 1](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole3.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pole3.webp)

Somehow, this pole didn't fall and wobbled back upright. The crowd went wild for the very young rider, and she actually finished with a really great time.

From the horse show we went over to the Miracle of Life barn to check out the changes since our visit on the first day of the Fair. Lots of new babies!! Henry had fun saying hello.

[![Piglet](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pig.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pig.webp)

[![Lamb](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/lamb.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/lamb.webp)

Naturally, shortly after visiting with the piglet, Henry wanted a pork chop. Circle of Life in action.

[![Pork Chop](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/chop.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/chop.webp)

Still delightful, but I do have to say this one was just a touch overdone. Luck of the draw in the chop line, you get what you get! It'll always be really good, and sometimes its transcendental. 

Across the street is the Fresh French Fries, and we were sitting on a token for a free cup of them. Luckily, the fries reverted back to the good Minnesota potatoes earlier in the Fair so these'll be a yummy treat.

[![Fresh French Fries](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/fries.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/fries.webp)

Wait a minute... those fries look suspicious. The color is off again. And Katie said the potatoes were covered up back in the kitchen behind the counter. They didn't... they wouldn't... they can't have... Tasting one only heightened my concern. I had to find out for sure. I snuck around back...

[![Boxes of Potatoes](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/potatoes.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/potatoes.webp)

**THEY SWITCHED BACK TO THE OUT OF STATE POTATOES AGAIN!!!** Big Lake potatoes come in sacks, not boxes. I later confirmed these are definitely from a California-based company that has no growing operations in Minnesota. **FRYGATE IS BACK!!!**

Really not cool. The shady covering of the potatoes is especially lame. Look, I get that you probably don't want to take a financial bath on a Fair's worth of potatoes that you already ordered. Maybe you were trying to recoup some of your losses. Or maybe you couldn't get enough Big Lake potatoes on short notice. Just be honest with your customers - "Hey, we heard you loud and clear, we'll definitely bring Big Lake potatoes back next year" would have been better than the way you handled this. Boo.

Investigative reporting complete, we headed across the street to the Dairy Building just to check out the butter sculptures. Congrats to Jena Hanson, chosen as Princess Kay of the Milky Way! She will now travel the state as an ambassador for the dairy industry over the next year. I don't actually know what that entails, but good for her!

[![Princess Kay](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/princess.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/princess.webp)

Nearby were the meat competition winners. Unsurprisingly, Henry was a big fan of the wall of meat! 

[![Meat Competition](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/meat.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/meat.webp)

As we exited the Dairy Building we found ourselves in front of the Haunted Mansion. Fifteen years into my fairgoing and I've never been in the Haunted Mansion. And I'd be perfectly happy keeping it that way, horror is not really my thing. But Henry, on the other hand... he loves creepy things and wanted to go in. So, in we went! 

[![Haunted Mansion](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/haunted.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/haunted.webp)

It was fun to be doing something new at the Fair after all these years. I didn't/couldn't take any pictures in there, so if you want to see it you'll just have to enter yourself! I will say, it's incredibly dark in there - I walked into two walls. It's not super scary, certainly more tame than the Halloween haunted house attractions I've been to. But definitely some "interesting" visuals and scenes throughout!

Katie wanted to do some shopping in the International Bazaar. Henry and I weren't super interested in that, but there happened to be a fun concert on the Bazaar stage at the same time so we set up shop there while Katie shopped. (Remember how this was going to be a quick visit? Ha!)

The band was The Drums of Navaronne, a local eight-piece Latin funk band. They were fun and high energy. Maybe a little too high energy? 

[![Pole Weaving 1](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/band.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/band.webp)

We listened to their whole set while Katie shopped. After, we cut through the Ag Building to get over to the Food Building. (What? We hadn't eaten in over an hour!) Henry liked the Ag Building centerpiece and wanted a photo with mom.

[![Ag Building Flowers](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/flowers.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/flowers.webp)

We wanted to try something different in the Food Building, and we picked Walleye On-a-Stick. Seafood at the Fair, I know, but Walleye is Lakefood so it makes more sense. And this stand has been there forever, so it can't be that bad!

[![Walleye On-a-Stick](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/walleyeonastick.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/walleyeonastick.webp)

We got the walleye on a stick (duh). 

[![Walleye](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/walleye.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/walleye.webp)

What a disappointment. The walleye itself was actually good if not great - nice that it's not like super coated and fried with tons of batter for one thing, it was lightly done which was nice. But the portion size for $8 - come on. The profit margin here is ridiculous. Cannot justify getting that again at that price.

Luckily, we also got a birch beer there, which was $3 for a large with the coupon book deal.

[![Birch Beer](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/birchbeer.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/birchbeer.webp)

This, on the other hand, was excellent (and cheap)! I am a sucker for birch beer having grown up near Pennsylvania Dutch country, so I always enjoy getting it out here when I see it. 

Henry's random pick from the food building was Manny's Tortas. We have been to their regular restaurant many times and those are excellent, but would it translate to the Fair? 

[![Torta](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/torta.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/torta.webp)

Henry wanted a ham one, and what's nice is that they sell them in halves and wholes. The half is like $7 and a decent amount (hey, Walleye on-a-stick just around the corner, take notes). It did seem maybe a little stripped down from what they do at the restaurant, but I had a bite and it was decent. If you want a sandwich at the Fair, you could do a lot worse (could that be....foreshadowing?) 

While near the Food Building Katie wanted to revisit a favorite treat from last year, the chocolate-dipped key lime pie from the kiosk out in the plaza.

[![Key Lime Pie](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pie.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pie.webp)

Same as last year, just as good as last year. Cold and refreshing and chocolatey.

Ok, so Katie's been talking about Al's Subs all Fair long, thinking it might be one of those random under-the-radar finds that is actually really good. She heard a little bit of buzz recently so finally decided to give them a try. She also opted for the ham. This was a $12 sub. 

[![Al's Subs](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/als.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/als.webp)

So, so, so very sad. Skimpy doesn't really describe it. More like "gave up even trying". Guess what? Al's is definitely NOT an under-the-radar cult Fair hit. And it's a bummer because a good sub is actually something that I think I'd like at the Fair if I was looking for something a bit more "normal" for lunch. But this ain't it. If you're looking for a sandwich at the fair, you actually can't do worse - this is the literal worst sandwich at the fair. Oh well.

We then split up, Katie for more shopping in the Grandstand and Henry and I towards the Midway. Now, I know this blog basically just looks like we are doing nothing but eating, but really the portions aren't large and there's also in-between time that isn't blog-worthy, so we were hungry for second lunch. We decided to try fajita's from Juanita's Fajitas. I've heard these are popular with fair workers. We got the combo, one steak and one chicken, which comes with the side of beans.

[![Fajita](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/fajita.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/fajita.webp)

These were actually pretty darn decent, especially the steak one. And I thought the combo deal wasn't outrageously expensive, just Fair expensive. These plus a Fresca to wash them down was actually surprisingly good.

You know what's a smart thing to do right after eating? A roller coaster that spins. Henry and I went on the Crazy Mouse.

[![Coaster](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/ride.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/ride.webp)

Luckily no post-lunch illness, just a lot of fun. Spin!! 

[![Coaster 2](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/spin.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/spin.webp)

Our "quick visit" was coming to an end seven hours after it started. There was just one thing left to do.

[![Fried Pickles](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pickles.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/pickles.webp)

Pickles from the Perfect Pickle stand on the way out. Next to the Mouth Trap curds these are probably the most consistent food at the Fair. You know what you're getting, they're the same every time. And they were good. Now, earlier in the Fair you'll recall that we tried the fried pickle fries at Mike's. It's hard to compare the two - they're texturally so, so different. These are definitely way crunchier, and totally different direction on the dipping sauce. I think there's room in my life for two different shapes of fried pickles, right? Basically different food groups altogether. So, we don't need to pick a winner.

And with that, Fair 2026 was a wrap. One last stamp, of course. 

[![Stamp](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/stamp.webp)](/assets/2026/2026Visit4/stamp.webp)

Four visits worth of fun, food, and Fair randomness. There are still two posts coming before 2026 is officially done, a crop art roundup and of course, the Fair Awards. I'll get those out over the next week or so. 

And only 347 more days until the 2027 Minnesota State Fair!
